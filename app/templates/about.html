About {{ app_name }}

An AI-powered mock interview and candidate assessment platform built for final year projects and real-world hiring preparation.

Technology Stack
  • Backend: Python 3.12, Flask, SQLAlchemy, JWT
  • Frontend: HTML5, Bootstrap 5, Chart.js, Jinja2
  • AI: Google Gemini, OpenCV, MediaPipe, SpeechRecognition
  • Database: SQLite (dev) / MySQL (production)
Modules
  1. Authentication with JWT & Google OAuth
  2. Resume PDF parsing & skill extraction
  3. AI interview question generation
  4. Live interview with webcam & microphone
  5. Speech analysis (grammar, pace, fillers)
  6. Emotion detection (OpenCV + MediaPipe)
  7. AI evaluation & scoring
  8. Role-based dashboards
  9. PDF report generation
